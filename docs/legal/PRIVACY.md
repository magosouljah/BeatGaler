# BeatGaler Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0**  
**Effective date: August 30, 2026**  
**Last updated: August 30, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how BeatGaler ("BeatGaler," "we," "us," or "the Service"), operated by Bruno Garcia, collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the BeatGaler desktop application, web services, and associated cloud features.

By using BeatGaler, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the Service.

## 1. Who We Are

BeatGaler is a software service for organizing, managing, playing, storing, and synchronizing music beats and related files.

**Data controller:** Bruno Garcia  
**Service:** BeatGaler  
**Country of operation:** Mexico  
**Contact:** support@beatgaler.com

BeatGaler is operated from Mexico and is intended to comply with applicable Mexican privacy law, including the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties, as applicable.

## 2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on the features you use.

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create or use a BeatGaler account, we may collect:

- Username
- Email address
- Account identifiers
- Authentication and session information
- Subscription or plan status

If you create a password, BeatGaler does not store the password in plain text. Passwords are stored using a salted cryptographic password-hashing process.

### 2.2 Google and X Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google or X, BeatGaler may receive information authorized by you and provided by that service.

For Google, this may include your Google account identifier, name, email address, and basic profile information.

For X, this may include your X account identifier, username, name, and basic profile information.

BeatGaler does not receive or store your Google or X password.

Authentication with these providers uses OAuth 2.0 or another supported authorization mechanism.

BeatGaler may store authentication tokens provided by these services when necessary to maintain the integration or provide the requested functionality.

### 2.3 Your BeatGaler Library and Uploaded Content

When you use BeatGaler cloud features, we may process and store content associated with your library, including:

- Audio files
- Beat names and metadata
- Artwork
- Project files or archives
- Samples and related files
- Tags, BPM, musical key, and other library information
- Technical identifiers required to synchronize your library across devices

You retain ownership of your content.

BeatGaler processes this content only as necessary to provide, maintain, synchronize, secure, and recover your library and related Service functionality.

### 2.4 Device and Technical Information

We may collect technical information necessary to operate and protect the Service, including:

- IP address
- Device or installation identifiers
- Operating system and application version
- Authentication and session information
- Request timestamps
- Error information
- Security events
- Performance and diagnostic logs

Diagnostic logging is designed not to intentionally include the contents of your music files, passwords, or private authentication secrets.

### 2.5 Subscription and Payment Information

If you purchase a paid BeatGaler plan, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider.

BeatGaler may receive information such as:

- Subscription status
- Plan
- Billing period
- Payment status
- Transaction or customer identifiers
- Refund or chargeback status

BeatGaler does not store your full credit or debit card number.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate BeatGaler, including creating and managing accounts; authenticating users; providing Google and X sign-in; storing and synchronizing libraries; operating subscriptions and plan limits; preventing fraud and abuse; diagnosing technical problems; securing accounts and infrastructure; providing customer support; improving reliability and performance; processing account deletion or privacy requests; and complying with applicable legal obligations.

**BeatGaler does not sell your personal information.**

We do not use your uploaded beats or project files to claim ownership of your music.

BeatGaler may use third-party service providers to operate parts of the Service, including cloud infrastructure, storage, authentication, payment processing, email delivery, monitoring, security, and related technical services.

These providers may process information only as necessary to perform the services for which they are used, subject to their applicable terms, privacy policies, and contractual obligations.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, investigate fraud or abuse, protect BeatGaler or its users, or protect the safety and security of the Service.

If BeatGaler is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or sale of substantially all relevant assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

## 5. International Data Processing

BeatGaler operates from Mexico but may use service providers or infrastructure located in other countries.

As a result, information may be transferred to or processed in jurisdictions outside your country of residence.

Where required, we take reasonable measures to use providers that maintain appropriate security and data-protection safeguards.

## 6. Data Security

BeatGaler uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect user information.

These measures may include encrypted network connections, cryptographic password hashing, access controls, restricted infrastructure credentials, authentication controls, backups, and security monitoring.

However, no online service, storage system, or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials and devices.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate your account and provide the Service, satisfy legal or accounting requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain security.

When an account or information is deleted, some information may remain temporarily in backups, logs, or disaster-recovery systems before being removed through normal retention cycles.

We may retain limited records when required by law or reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, billing disputes, or legal claims.

## 8. Account and Content Deletion

You may request deletion of your BeatGaler account and associated personal information by contacting:

**support@beatgaler.com**

Where account-deletion functionality is available directly within BeatGaler, you may also use that functionality.

Deletion requests are subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and backup-retention requirements.

Canceling a paid subscription does **not** automatically delete your BeatGaler account or library.

Downgrading to a lower plan does **not** delete your existing BeatGaler library.
